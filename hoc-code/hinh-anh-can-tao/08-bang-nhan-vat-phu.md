# Bảng nhân vật phụ (4 nhân vật, một hàng)

**Lưu thành:** một hình lớn, sau đó cắt thành 4 file trong `assets/mascot/`:
- `nv01-bit.png` (robot Bit)
- `nv02-bo-loi.png` (Bọ Lỗi)
- `nv03-mui-ten-ptr.png` (Mũi Tên Ptr)
- `nv04-goi-y.png` (Đèn Gợi Ý)

## Prompt
Generate an image: A character model sheet on a plain white background, four characters side by side in one row, equal panels separated by thin light grey lines, same flat vector style as a friendly learning app: thin dark navy #1C2027 outline of uniform weight, rounded soft shapes, palette limited to orange #F08A4B, cream #F5F2EA, navy #1C2027, blue #2F5FB3 and brick red #B8521F accents only. Left to right: (1) "Bit", a small friendly round robot with a blue visor showing a smile, relaxed, one hand raised with a small glowing lamp; (2) "Bọ Lỗi", a cute round beetle with a brick red shell and tiny legs, looking a bit embarrassed but cheerful, holding a small magnifying glass; (3) "Mũi Tên Ptr", a small cute arrow character with two round eyes and a small flag on top, pointing forward to show it points to an address; (4) "Đèn Gợi Ý", a small cute lightbulb character with a friendly face and a soft glow, relaxed. Each character is centered in its own equal panel. No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no watermark, no sparkle or star in a corner, no realistic textures, no 3D render, no gradients, no dark background.

## Negative prompt
text, letters, numbers, labels, watermark, sparkle, star, realistic textures, 3D render, gradient, dark background, cat, kitten

## Ghi chú
- Không có mèo Miu trong bảng này. Mèo Miu có bảng riêng (6 biểu cảm).
- Nhân vật có thể lệch chi tiết nhỏ so với mô tả. Kiểm tra trước khi cắt.
